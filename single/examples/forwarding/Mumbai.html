Mumbai

Mumbai, sprawling along the west coast of India, is the capital of Maharashtra and the financial powerhouse of the country. Originally a cluster of seven islands inhabited by the Koli fishing community, massive colonial-era land reclamation unified them into a high-density mega-city that is now home to an estimated metropolitan population of over 2.2 crore (22 million) people. As the heart of Indian commerce, it houses the country's most vital financial institutions—including the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), and the National Stock Exchange (NSE)—alongside the multi-billion dollar Hindi film industry, globally famed as Bollywood.

The city is defined by a captivating architectural duality, seamlessly transitioning from the grand, Victorian Gothic-style UNESCO World Heritage sites of South Mumbai—like Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus and the iconic Gateway of India—to soaring modern skyscrapers and the engineering marvel of the Bandra-Worli Sea Link. Driven by its lifeline network of local trains, Mumbai maintains a relentless, fast-paced energy famously summarized by the phrase "the city that never sleeps." It is a cultural melting pot where premier lifestyle districts like Bandra coexist alongside Asia's largest street-food hubs, offering everything from elite sea-facing fine dining to the universal local staple, vada pav.
