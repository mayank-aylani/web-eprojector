Ujjain

Ujjain is an ancient and sacred city in Madhya Pradesh, India, situated on the banks of the Kshipra River. Known historically as Ujjayini and the capital of the ancient Avanti kingdom, it is celebrated as one of Hinduism’s seven holy Sapta Puri cities and houses the famous Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga temple, featuring a unique south-facing swayambhu (self-manifested) lingam.

The city also plays a massive role in Indian astronomy and timekeeping, as the Tropic of Cancer passes through it, and it served as the prime meridian for ancient Indian astronomers—home to the historic Vedh Shala (Jantar Mantar) observatory built in the 18th century. Every 12 years, Ujjain draws millions of pilgrims from across the globe when it hosts the Simhastha Kumbh Mela along the sacred river ghats
