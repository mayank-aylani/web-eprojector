Bangalore

Bengaluru (formerly Bangalore), positioned on the Deccan Plateau in southern India, is the capital of Karnataka and the undisputed technological capital of the country, widely celebrated as the Silicon Valley of India. Historically established by Kempe Gowda I in 1537, the city transformed during the mid-20th century into India's public-sector aerospace and heavy industries hub before sparking the nation's IT revolution in the 1980s and 90s. Today, it hosts thousands of global technology corporations, massive R&D centres, and a hyper-dynamic startup ecosystem that breeds the highest number of tech unicorns in India.

Sitting at an elevation of over 900 metres, Bengaluru enjoys a famously moderate, pleasant climate year-round, which earned it the historical moniker of the "Garden City" due to sprawling green lungs like Lalbagh Botanical Garden and Cubbon Park. The city is a vibrant cultural melting pot with a massive student and young professional population, driving a legendary lifestyle scene renowned as the pub and microbrewery capital of India. From its tech parks in Whitefield and Electronic City to its traditional core in Malleshwaram and Basavanagudi, Bengaluru seamlessly balances old-world charm, South Indian culinary institutions like vidyarthi bhavan, and forward-looking cosmopolitan energy.
