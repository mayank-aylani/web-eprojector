Indore

Indore, located just 55 kilometres south of Ujjain, is the largest city and the commercial capital of Madhya Pradesh. Globally celebrated for its civic pride, Indore has famously held the title of India's cleanest city for eight consecutive years in the national Swachh Survekshan awards, while also securing the second spot nationwide in the recent Swachh Vayu Survekshan for clean air. Historically governed by the Holkar dynasty under the visionary Rani Ahilyabai Holkar, the city seamlessly blends heritage sites like the grand Rajwada Palace and Lal Bagh Palace with a booming modern economy.

Beyond its commercial might, Indore is a premier educational epicentre, uniquely hosting both an Indian Institute of Technology (IIT Indore) and an Indian Institute of Management (IIM Indore). For food lovers, it is considered the street-food capital of central India, renowned for its late-night food markets like Sarafa Bazar and the bustling Chappan Dukaan, where crowds gather daily to enjoy local delicacies like poha-jalebi, bhutte ka kees, and khopra patties.
